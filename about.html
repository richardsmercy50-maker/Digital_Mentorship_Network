About SheConnect

SheConnect is a digital mentorship platform created by devQueens under TECH4DEV. Our mission is to empower young women by connecting them with mentors, accountability partners, and a supportive community that guides them through education, career, and life.

Our Roles

Lead Developers

Build and maintain the platform.

UI/UX Designers

Create functional, beautiful user experiences.

Content & Community

Manage student resources and community growth.

QA & Testing

Ensure everything runs smoothly and bug-free.
